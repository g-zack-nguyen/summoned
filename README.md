# Summoned

An original offline monster-card duel game in two worlds, mythic fantasy and the Day 1 office: classic rules, a campaign of CPU duelists, card packs and a deck builder.

**Play:** https://g-zack-nguyen.github.io/summoned/

It plays in any current browser - Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Edge - on a phone or a computer.

## On an iPhone

1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. No Share button (iOS 26)? Tap **•••** first, then **Share**.
3. Open Summoned from the Home Screen once while you are online. After that it plays offline too.

**Add it to the Home Screen before you play, and play from there.** The Home Screen app and a Safari tab keep
separate saves (the app starts empty: a tab's save moves with a save code, see below), and Safari may delete the
save of a tab you have not visited for 7 days.

## Two worlds

Summoned plays in two worlds with the same cards and rules: **Fantasy**, the engraved world of dragons and knights,
and **Day 1**, a bright glass office tower where the same cards are coworkers, actions and surprises. Pick one on the
first screen, and switch any time with **World** on Home or in Settings. Each world keeps its own progress; your name
and your sound settings are shared. Both worlds play offline.

## Moving your progress

Your progress is saved on the device only. To move it to another browser or device, open **Settings** and tap
**Copy save code**. Then open Summoned where you want to play: on its first screen tap **Have a save code? Import it**
(or, if you already play there, go to **Settings** and tap **Import save code**) and paste the code. A code holds the
progress of one world. Before it replaces anything, the game shows which world's progress it will replace and asks you
first.

## Offline and private

There is no account, no ads and no tracking: the game never sends anything anywhere. Every card, picture and sound is
made in code. After the first visit it plays offline. When a new version is out, the game says **New version ready**
with an **Update now** button; your progress stays.
